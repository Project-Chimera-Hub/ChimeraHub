Prophantasia

Seeing on purpose, built up in stages. It starts with the one thing the eye already makes on its own, the afterimage: first keeping it, then steering it. Only then flashed images, held in the mind or projected onto a blank field, and last, images made from a description alone. Rate each hold; the level follows the ratings, and as it rises the images get more detailed and what counts as holding one gets stricter.
