Listening Integration

Relational integration by ear. No buttons during a round: listen, decide silently, then hear the answer.

How it works

You hear a stream of spoken numbers. Each one also has a place (left to right in your headphones) and a pitch (lower to higher).

From the (n+2)th sound on, compare two relations: the one between the previous sound and the sound n+1 back, and the one between the current sound and the sound n back. Decide silently: same relation or not?

After a short silence a tone gives the answer: two rising notes for a match, one low note for different. Some non-matches are built to sound right: off by one, right with the wrong partner, or the right size the wrong way.

The other two qualities keep changing at random. Ignore them, unless the round compares across them.

Headphones needed for place. Once it plays, the screen can be locked; it stops when you leave it through the hub.
