Training archive

One record across every trainer. The file is the archive — this page only maintains it. Nothing a browser holds survives clearing site data, so the copy that matters is the one you download and keep.

Drop exports here — or the archive itself, to restore it.

Syllogimous backups and RNB exports are recognised by their shape, not their name.

Ability

How this is read

Where the whole record stands, on six bands of roughly similar proficiency — α to ζ, from the Guanxinandu S11 benchmark, which is used here for its calibration rather than its test protocol: it says what "about this good" looks like in five trainers' own units, and nothing else supplies that. Each trainer is read against its own anchor, in its own units; nothing averages one app's difficulty with another's.

Where a trainer keeps its own ability estimate, that is what is read — RNB's best cleared load, Syllogimous's ability posterior, eWMT's best n. Each has already applied its own target accuracy and its own chance correction, and those differ: RNB scores a block so that never pressing is 0 and perfect is 1, where about 0.5 is four answers in five. So accuracy is not second-guessed here, and there is no bar to clear. A thin reading is reported thin and weighed light; it is never withheld, and no one trainer's settings can suppress the rest.

    Sources

    The year

    One square per day. A filled square is a day with training; an empty one is a rest day inside a stretch some export vouched for; a hatched one is a day nobody has evidence about — usually because site data was cleared before a backup was taken. Drawing the last two alike would claim you rested through exactly the weeks the record was lost.

    Over time

    Minutes as bars, difficulty as the line — because an adaptive trainer holds accuracy at a target and moves the difficulty until it gets there. A flat accuracy line means the controller is working and says nothing about whether you improved; what improved is whatever had to rise to keep it flat. Accuracy is in each day's tooltip, where it belongs.

    Overlap

    Comparing two trainers needs weeks in which both were played. Until there are enough, this counts them and says nothing else — with a handful of days, the strongest correlation between two unrelated measures still looks convincing, so a number here would mislead exactly when it was least earned.

    Notes

    Everything the trainers cannot tell you — a test score, an illness, a change of dose, a month you felt slow. None of it is in any export, and all of it is what makes a two-year series readable. Notes live in the archive file with the records, and in no export anywhere: this file or nowhere.
